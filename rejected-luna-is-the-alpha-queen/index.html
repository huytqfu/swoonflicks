Why watch

Arya buried her strength the day her pack fell, and she has been playing small ever since. This is the werewolf drama for anyone who has waited years for a quiet heroine to finally stop apologizing. The slow burn of her patience is the whole point, and when it snaps it is worth it.

  • The hidden-power trope done properly: the reveal is earned, not handed out in episode one.
  • A betrayal you will want to yell at, with an ex-mate and his new partner who make it easy to pick a side.
  • A second chance at love with the Lycan King, kept in the background long enough to feel like a reward.
  • Good pick if you liked rejected-mate or Luna stories and want revenge with a romantic payoff.
The story

After her Moonshadow Pack was slaughtered by rogues, Arya, the werewolf warrior, hides her immense power to honor her mother's dying wish. She marries Alpha Micah, and hides her strength. But then, Alpha Micah breaks his vows and returns from war with a new mate, Vera, and demands that Arya share her Luna title with her. Faced with Vera, Micah, and his family's cruelty, Arya can no longer bear it. She leaves Micah and unleashes the immense power of the White Wolf, reclaiming what is hers with the help of Lowe, the Lycan King. After separating from Micah, Arya discoveres that Vera not only stole Micah from her, but is also the traitor who slaughtered her parents and the Moonshadow Pack. Arya rushes to the battlefield to face her betrayers, and in the process, finds love in Lowe, the Lycan King.
