Why watch

A pediatric nurse wakes up in a dragon kingdom and discovers she can hear what baby dragons are thinking. It is the coziest drama on this list: less war, more problem-solving, with modern nursing know-how turning a palace maid into someone the royal family cannot do without.

  • A fresh isekai twist: the heroine's superpower is understanding a baby dragon.
  • Feel-good and low-stress, built on small rescues instead of brutal revenge.
  • A rise from maid to trusted royal nanny, with a rival who gets outplayed.
  • Good pick for time-travel and wish-fulfillment fans who want something light.
The story

Pediatric nurse Ava is transported to a dragon kingdom, where she can hear baby dragons’ thoughts. Using modern nursing skills, she saves troubled prince Knox from hidden injuries, allergies, and a deadly choking crisis, wins the Dragon King’s trust, defeats an old-guard rival, and rises from maid to the royal family’s top dragon nanny.
